Our story

From the back of the room to building my own.

The honest version: the pain, the people, and the purpose behind Omar Huertas LLC.

I was born into technology. I built my first computer at seven, while my stepfather worked for Wang, Northern Telecom, and Oxford. Growing up Puerto Rican on Long Island, I learned early what it means to be underestimated. The aptitude was always there; the world just needed convincing.

So I set out to prove it. At 31 I went back to school and earned an Associate of Applied Science in Network Technology, then stacked the certifications to back it: Cisco CCNA, CompTIA A+, Lean Six Sigma, ServiceNow, and Microsoft. Not because the work needed them, but because the world asked for proof.

A few people saw it before the paper did: Deena, Brian, and many others who bet on me early. Deena believed in me long before I had any proof. Brian gave me my first real opportunity. Mike Lombardo, the Mayor of ServiceNow, showed me the standard, and the GlideFast community he built showed me what it means to treat people right. I’ve carried that into every engagement since, delivering on billion-dollar enterprise platforms.

Now I’ve started my own: Omar Huertas LLC. Not for the biggest logo in the room, but for honest work, done right, for the people and teams the industry overlooks. Skill first. Honest work always.

Omar HuertasFounder & ServiceNow Architect

Why we exist

More than a company. A movement.

I was meant for this. Everything that tried to take me out failed, because there was purpose in it.

Omar Huertas LLC isn’t just consulting. It’s a movement: to help the helpless, mentor the overlooked, and give hope to the ones who stopped believing they had it, because the world made them doubt their own truth and talent.

I started this because I’ve watched it up close: a friend who outworks everyone, more than skilled, with a visa sponsorship held over his head like a leash, denied the break he earned. Talent should never be hostage to status, a name, or where you came from.

We back the deserving. We give the shot. On skill, nothing more, nothing less.

The journey

The road, honestly.

Nine moments that made the standard.

  1. Age 7

    Built my first computer

    My stepfather worked for Wang, Northern Telecom, and Oxford. Tech was in the house before I knew it was rare, on Long Island, where a Puerto Rican kid was told he didn’t belong.

  2. 2004

    Where it began: Deena

    The real start, long before ServiceNow. Deena was the beginning of everything that came after.

  3. Age 31

    Back to school: degree and certifications

    Earned an Associate of Applied Science in Network Technology, plus CCNA, A+, Lean Six Sigma, ServiceNow, and Microsoft. Proof on paper of what was always there.

  4. 2015

    The first chance: Brian at DD

    Brian didn’t see where I came from. He saw the CCNA and the work, and took me for real. The first yes. I’ve never forgotten it.

  5. 2017

    First ServiceNow Knowledge, Orlando

    Walked in as a nobody and fell in love with the platform and the community. Never missed a year since.

  6. 2018–2023

    Architect at two top-tier ServiceNow partners

    CMDB, ITOM, ITSM, integrations, and automation, delivered at real enterprise scale, year over year.

  7. The mentor

    Learned the standard from the Mayor

    In the GlideFast community, Mike Lombardo, the Mayor of ServiceNow, gave talent a chance and treated everyone like they mattered. That became my standard.

  8. $1B+

    Trusted at carrier scale

    Brought in by leading partners to deliver on billion-dollar platforms for some of the largest telecom carriers in the world.

  9. 2026, today

    Founded Omar Huertas LLC

    Registered ServiceNow Build Partner. Just registered, just beginning: betting on myself, and carrying the legacy forward.
