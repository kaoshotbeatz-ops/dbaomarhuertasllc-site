Obvera: IT operations with a change record behind every action.

One service for ITSM, MDM, procurement, security and data, built for small businesses, non-profits and growing teams. In development; not yet generally available.

Request early accessOur ServiceNow services
In development

How it works

Nothing changes off the record.

Each change is requested, approved by a person, executed once and closed, and every write is audited.

What it covers

One service instead of five tools.

The areas Obvera is designed to bring together.

ITSM
Incidents, requests and changes in one place.
MDM
Endpoint and device management.
Procurement
Requests, approvals and purchasing.
Security
Controls and evidence that stay with the record.
Data
One source of truth for assets and people.

Obvera works alongside ServiceNow, which remains our core practice.
